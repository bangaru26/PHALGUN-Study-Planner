Assessment and answer-release rules

  • MCQs and assignments are original practice, not copied exam questions.
  • Answer keys and rationales remain hidden until you submit your answers.
  • After submission, review includes your answer, correct answer, explanation, why alternatives are weaker, and a targeted revision recommendation.
  • Assessment scores do not automatically mean mastery; practical evidence and repeated application matter.
  • Credentials such as CMA, CIA, CPA, EA, Scrum or other qualifications remain separate external credentials; always use the awarding body's current syllabus and eligibility rules.
