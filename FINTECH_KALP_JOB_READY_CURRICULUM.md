# Fintech Kalp — Job-Ready Curriculum & Handover Gate
**Version:** 1.1 · **Prepared:** 3 November 2026 · **Status:** curriculum map committed; app validation/handover still gated

Fintech Kalp is the focused finance + technology pathway within GFPA/PHALGUN. The learner target is employability in finance operations, accounting, reporting, analytics and junior finance transformation roles. This is an original learning plan, not an awarding-body certification or job guarantee.

## Learning contract
Each lesson uses: **Understand → Learn → Worked example → Guided practice → Independent task → Check → Portfolio evidence → Interview transfer**.
- Learner-facing lessons explain jargon plainly and show calculations step-by-step.
- Each quiz is chapter-specific. Learner submits every question (100% completion gate); scoring and answer rationale appear after submission.
- Suggested pass target: 80%; retry wrong answers with fresh questions/examples. Completion and score are distinct.
- Each course has a formula/terms quick reference and concise lesson notes integrated in relevant lessons.
- Laws, tax, IFRS, CMA and vendor software references use official primary sources, dated and jurisdiction-tagged. No affiliation implied or paid question banks copied.
- Fictional datasets only; protect customer/personal data. Preserve local export/backup and disclose browser-local persistence.
- Flexible suggested pace: 90–120 minutes/day, 5 days/week; checkpoints over deadline pressure.

## Employability-first pathway (12 blocks)
Sequential blocks; duration estimates at 5–7 hours/week.

### Block 0 — Career baseline & digital workflow (1 week)
Role map: AP, AR, R2R, GL, reconciliations, finance analyst, reporting analyst, FP&A, finance systems. Resume inventory, job-description keyword extraction, Office file hygiene, business email, communication. Deliverable: target-role shortlist, diagnostic and skills-gap tracker.

### Block 1 — Accounting & finance foundations (2 weeks)
Accounting equation; double entry; debit/credit; journals, ledgers, trial balance. Accruals, prepayments, provisions, depreciation, fixed assets and inventory basics. Financial statements/linkages, close calendar and materiality. AP/P2P, AR/O2C, R2R, bank/control-account reconciliations, ageing, working capital. Practice 25 journal transactions, trial balance, three-way match exceptions and AR ageing. Portfolio: month-end close pack with reconciliation and variance commentary.

### Block 2 — Advanced Excel for finance (2 weeks)
Tables, references, names, data types, validation, conditional formatting, error handling. High-frequency formulas: SUM/SUMIFS, COUNT/COUNTIFS, IF/IFS, IFERROR, AND/OR, XLOOKUP, INDEX/MATCH, FILTER, SORT, UNIQUE, TEXT, DATE, EOMONTH, ROUND, SUBTOTAL, LET (where supported). PivotTables/PivotCharts, charts, slicers, dynamic arrays, formula auditing and model controls. Finance models: AP ageing, bank reconciliation, budget-vs-actual, cash forecast, close tracker. Macro/VBA literacy: recorder, procedures, variables, ranges, loops, conditions, workbook events, error handling, safe macros, trust settings and debugging. Never bypass approvals/security. Excel dictionary covers frequently used finance formulas, patterns, examples, pitfalls and practical macro patterns—not every function. Portfolio: controlled finance workbook with documentation, test cases and macro demo.

### Block 3 — Data cleaning & Power Query (1 week)
Source profiling, null/duplicate handling, type conversion, standardization, merge/append, unpivot, transformations, refresh and reconciliation. ETL controls: row counts, control totals, exception logs, lineage and reproducibility. Portfolio: reusable AP/GL refresh pipeline.

### Block 4 — Power BI & reporting (2 weeks)
Star schema, fact/dimension tables, relationships, grain, date table, measures vs calculated columns. DAX: SUM, CALCULATE, FILTER, DIVIDE, DISTINCTCOUNT, date intelligence and context fundamentals. KPI definitions, variance/ageing visuals, drill-through, report usability, refresh governance and access controls. Portfolio: finance dashboard with metric dictionary and stakeholder narrative.

### Block 5 — SQL for analysts (2 weeks)
SELECT, aliases, WHERE, ORDER BY, CASE, NULLs, GROUP BY, HAVING. JOINs, subqueries, CTEs, UNION, date/text functions, window functions (ROW_NUMBER, SUM OVER, LAG). Data quality, reconciliation, duplicate detection, safe read-only practice. Portfolio: 15 documented finance SQL queries over fictional AP/AR/GL data.

### Block 6 — Python for finance data (1 week)
Variables, types, conditions, loops, functions, collections, CSV, pandas basics, cleaning, groupby, merge, validation and charts. Portfolio: repeatable AP ageing/exception analysis.

### Block 7 — SAP FICO & ERP process orientation (2 weeks)
ERP concepts, master data, company code/chart of accounts, FI-GL, AP, AR, asset accounting, bank accounting and CO cost centres. P2P/O2C integration, document flow, posting periods, approvals, roles and segregation of duties. Version-specific screens/configuration: use training/demo environments; don't imply access to a live tenant. Portfolio: process map, sample journal/document walkthrough, control matrix and interview answers.

### Block 8 — IFRS & financial reporting literacy (2 weeks, ongoing updates)
Conceptual framework, IAS 1/7/8/10/12/16/21/36/37/38, IFRS 9/15/16 overview. Recognition, measurement, presentation, disclosure, estimates and journal impacts. Cite current authoritative IASB material; maintain version/date and jurisdiction notes. Portfolio: accounting position memo on fictional case with official citations.

### Block 9 — Treasury, FP&A & finance operations (2 weeks)
Cash positioning, liquidity, 13-week cash forecast, bank reconciliations, payment controls, FX basics, working capital. Budget, rolling forecast, drivers, price-volume-mix, scenario/sensitivity, variance analysis and commentary. Shared-services KPIs, SLA/OLA, capacity, queues, root cause, RACI and improvement. Portfolio: forecast, KPI pack, variance narrative and process proposal.

### Block 10 — AI, automation, controls & fintech (1 week)
AI/GenAI fundamentals, prompt patterns, grounding, evaluation, hallucinations, privacy and human review. RPA/OCR/API/workflows, retries, idempotency, logging, monitoring, access and exception queues. Payments, digital banking, lending, wallets, open banking, fraud/AML concepts, cybersecurity and operational risk. Portfolio: controlled invoice-exception triage design with human approvals retained.

### Block 11 — Job readiness & capstone (2 weeks)
Role-tailored resume, achievement statements, Naukri/LinkedIn profile, application tracker, outreach. Interview drills: accounting entries, Excel test, SQL scenario, dashboard story, SAP process, controls, STAR behavioral. Capstone: fictional-company month-end and finance analytics pack—journals, reconciliation, Excel analysis, SQL checks, Power BI dashboard, process/control note and executive presentation. Rubric: accuracy 30%, controls/evidence 20%, analysis 20%, communication 15%, reproducibility 15%. Portfolio includes resume, workbook, SQL, dashboard, process map, memo, capstone and interview answer bank.

## Lesson and assessment inventory minimum
Each block has chapter landing page, objectives/prerequisites, 4–8 granular lessons, original teaching notes, worked examples, dictionary links, guided practice, independent exercise, chapter quiz, rationales, flashcards/revision, portfolio artefact and references. No placeholder cards marked complete.

## Dictionary integration specification
- Accounting: terms, journal patterns, close/reconciliation/control vocabulary.
- Excel: common function families, finance use cases, syntax, worked input/output, edge cases, errors; safe VBA patterns and explanation.
- Power Query/BI: transformations, model concepts, DAX patterns and KPI definitions.
- SQL: clauses/functions/operators, joins/window patterns with finance examples.
- Python: terms, pandas methods and reproducible snippets.
- SAP FICO: modules, organization/master data, process/document flow.
- IFRS: standards and terms with authoritative source/version date.
All entries searchable/filterable and deep-linked from lessons; do not aim for an unrealistic exhaustive catalog.

## Release and handover gates (evidence required)
1. Flutter pub get succeeds and analyzer has no errors.
2. Release web build succeeds on exact candidate commit.
3. Pages artifact/base-href routing verified; no fatal console/runtime errors on Android Chrome and Windows browser.
4. Test navigation, dictionary links, 100% quiz completion gate, scoring/rationale, timer, progress, notes, backup/restore and offline shell.
5. Confirm published URL and learner guide. Windows EXE is separate; do not claim an EXE unless generated and tested.
6. Record run links, commit SHA, test results and known limits. Until gates pass, label candidate—not complete handover.

## Immediate study-start sequence
Begin Block 1 accounting foundations and Block 2 Excel in parallel (30–45 minutes each/day), practice with fictional AP/GL data, then progress in sequence. Do not wait for all future lessons to be written before starting.
