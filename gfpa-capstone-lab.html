03 · Month-end close

Complete a bank reconciliation

Bank statement balance: ₹1,85,400. Cash book balance before updates: ₹1,90,300. Deposit in transit: ₹12,000. Unpresented cheque: ₹7,500. Bank charges not yet in the cash book: ₹600. Bank interest not yet recorded: ₹200.

Show the working after you attempt it

Adjusted bank: ₹1,85,400 + ₹12,000 deposit in transit − ₹7,500 unpresented cheque = ₹1,89,900.

Adjusted cash book: ₹1,90,300 − ₹600 bank charges + ₹200 interest = ₹1,89,900. Record the missing cash-book entries and retain supporting evidence.

Deliverable

Write your close-out note

Before closing this case, write a short note covering:

  • What you would hold, verify or escalate—and why.
  • Which evidence you would retain for audit trail.
  • Which cash-book entries must be posted and independently reviewed.
  • One preventive control to reduce repeat exceptions.
