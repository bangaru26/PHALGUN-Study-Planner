Review and versioning policy

  • Keep old lesson versions available for historical study.
  • Separate “source page changed” from “rule confirmed changed”.
  • For legal/accounting updates, cite the official notification or standard, jurisdiction, publication date, effective date and applicability.
  • For Excel, Power BI, Python, SQL and SAP, record product/version differences and deprecations; never generalise one product's behaviour to every version.
  • “Remains the same” must name the exact checked source and date. It is not a blanket assurance.

Scheduled checks are configured daily at 02:20 UTC (07:50 IST), with weekly cadence guidance for product/learning documentation in the source registry. The first successful run establishes the baseline.
