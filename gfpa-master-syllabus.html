Standard chapter learning package

  1. Prerequisites, outcomes, vocabulary and estimated time.
  2. Original beginner-to-practitioner explanations, formulas and concepts.
  3. Worked examples with line-by-line reasoning and realistic fictional data.
  4. Guided practical demonstration and independent assignment.
  5. Cross-functional impacts, quality checks, risks and controls.
  6. MCQs and applied questions; answers and explanations stay hidden until submission.
  7. Flashcards, revision gist, formula sheet and previous/next/related links.
  8. Topic-block mini-capstone with deliverables, acceptance criteria and rubric.
  9. Official/authoritative resources with dates, URLs, status and license metadata.
  10. Printable lesson, worksheet, submitted-answer report and capstone export.

13-domain master map

01 · Accounting & Finance Core

Statements, journals, accruals, AP/AR, P2P/O2C/R2R, close, reconciliations, working capital, costing and controls.

02 · Global Finance Operations

Shared services/GCCs, SLAs, KPIs, capacity, exception management, SOPs, governance and continuous improvement.

03 · FP&A & Business Partnering

Budgets, rolling forecasts, driver-based planning, variance, scenarios, cash forecasting and management storytelling.

04 · AI-Enabled Finance

GenAI, prompting, use-case selection, evaluation, data handling, human review, logs and governance.

05 · Data & Analytics

Advanced Excel, Power Query, Power BI/DAX, SQL, Python, data modelling, quality and storytelling.

06 · Automation & Intelligent Operations

RPA, workflows, OCR/IDP, APIs, exception queues, monitoring, controls, ROI and testing.

07 · Agile & Scrum

Agile/Scrum, backlog, user stories, estimation, Kanban, Lean, metrics, facilitation and delivery.

08 · Finance Transformation

Process mapping, Lean/Six Sigma, DMAIC, root causes, change, governance and benefits realisation.

SoD, control testing, audit trails, access, fraud, privacy, resilience, compliance and remediation.

Role-based orientation to US CMA, CIA, EA/US tax, CPA pathways, IFRS and selected risk credentials; use official current requirements.

11 · GCC, Product Engineering & Digital Business

Operating models, product lifecycle, APIs, payments, digital economics, reliability and cross-functional collaboration.

12 · Career & Professional School

CV/LinkedIn, portfolios, interviews, case studies, executive communication and career strategy.

Diagnose a finance process, analyse data, design controls, plan Agile delivery, quantify value and present recommendations.

One cumulative fictional business case

A fictional company faces late supplier payments, unreliable cash forecasts and rising month-end reconciliation exceptions. Learners connect AP, Procurement, Receiving, Treasury, GL/R2R, AR, FP&A, IT/Data, Risk/Audit and management to diagnose causes, validate data, strengthen controls, prioritise improvements and measure benefits. All case facts and datasets should be original and invented for teaching.

Final capstone deliverables

  • Problem statement, baseline, assumptions and process map.
  • Clean dataset, data dictionary, analysis and root-cause evidence.
  • Risk/control matrix, future-state process and exception handling.
  • Prioritised backlog, sprint/release plan and adoption approach.
  • KPI dashboard and definitions; benefits model with assumptions.
  • Automation/AI recommendation with human controls and fallback.
  • Executive recommendation, sources, reflection and interview-ready case story.

Online, offline, save and export requirements

  • Online: responsive searchable PWA, accessible navigation, deep links and version labels.
  • Offline: cache the app shell and explicitly selected learning packs; allow lessons, local datasets, drafts and practice to work offline. Show last update and sync state.
  • Local-first records: autosave answers and drafts; allow export/backup and validated restore. Do not promise cross-device sync unless authenticated cloud sync is implemented.
  • Download/print: lesson and notes, formula sheet, flashcards, blank worksheet, submitted answers with feedback, capstone brief/submission, rubric and portfolio. Browser print-to-PDF is the fallback when direct PDF generation is unavailable.
  • Answer privacy: never include hidden answer keys in pre-submission exports; export only selected records and mark drafts/finals with dates and versions.

Copyright-safe content policy

  • Write teaching explanations, examples, fictional cases, questions, rubrics and datasets from scratch.
  • Do not copy textbooks, paid courseware, proprietary slides or exam-bank questions.
  • Explain facts and formulas independently; cite official legal, regulatory, standards and credential sources with dates and jurisdiction.
  • Track provenance: original / public domain / licensed / linked reference, author, source, license and review date.
  • For third-party images or material, use assets with compatible permission and retain attribution. Do not imply endorsement by vendors or credential bodies.

Regulatory update and syllabus governance

For each meaningful change, record jurisdiction, issuing authority, official source, publication/effective dates, status (proposed, enacted/published, in force or repealed), affected lesson IDs, verification date, reviewer and action taken. Patch only affected lessons and questions. If the official source does not confirm a claim, mark it unverified and do not rewrite the syllabus.

Release gates

A chapter is not complete just because an outline exists. It must pass factual and calculation review, chapter/assessment alignment, hidden-answer test, source/license review, accessibility/mobile review, offline test, save/restore test, print/export test, stable-link test and deployment smoke test.

Delivery sequence: canonical IDs and governance → offline/export foundations → three fully authored pilot chapters → six Fintech Kalp pathways → remaining GFPA domains and integrated capstones.

Current implementation status

This page documents the syllabus and acceptance criteria. It does not claim that every lesson, offline sync function, PDF generator or answer-export workflow is already implemented. Each capability must be built and tested before being described as live.
